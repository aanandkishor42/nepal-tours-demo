Ask us anything
How do I pay, and is an advance required?

We accept bank transfer, mobile wallets (eSewa/Khalti), cards and cash. A 20% advance confirms your booking; the balance is payable at the start or end of the trip, whichever you prefer.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel 7+ days before travel for a full refund of the advance. 48 hours–7 days: 50% refund. Within 48 hours the advance is non-refundable, though we'll always try to reschedule your trip instead.

Do you arrange trekking and park permits?

Yes. We can arrange TIMS cards, restricted-area permits (such as Mustang), Chitwan park entry and vehicle permits. Permit costs are quoted separately and never added silently.

Are your vehicles insured and drivers licensed?

Absolutely. Every vehicle has valid insurance, fitness and route permits; every driver holds a professional licence and has at least five years of mountain-road experience. We share documents on request.

How much luggage can I bring?

Sedans take 2 medium bags, SUVs 4 large bags, vans 12–16 bags and coaches 35+. For motorbikes we provide saddle bags. Roof racks are available on request for jeep journeys.
